## Validated choices (2026-10-24)

| Area | Choice | Canvas frame |
|---|---|---|
| Space screen | **1A**: Zen-style sidebar, full screen. From top: header, Essentials grid, pinned tabs and folders, divider, regular tabs, space dots, search field and + button | 1A |
| Changing space | **2A**: paged horizontal swipe; the list follows the finger and the colour blends between spaces | 2A |
| Opening a tab | **3A**: the tab row zooms into the page | 3A |
| Opened page | Full screen, with a single bottom bar showing the tab title and the domain | 5 |
| Back to the space / new tab | **4A**: drag the bottom bar up. Stop 1 goes back to the space, stop 2 opens a new tab | 4A |

The B and C options stay on the canvas for reference only.

## Visual direction (from the mockups, to be refined)
- Each space has its own accent colour and a dark background tinted with it. The mockups use Personal `#9B8CFF` on `#1D1A2B`, Work `#F0A060` on `#2A1F17`, and Research `#5CC8BE` on `#142528`.
- Type is the system font (SF Pro). Space name 22 pt bold, tab rows 16 pt, section labels 12 pt uppercase.
- Tab rows are 44 pt tall with 12 pt corner radius. Essentials tiles are 60 pt with 16 pt radius. The bottom bar is a 50–56 pt pill.
- Light mode is not designed yet.
